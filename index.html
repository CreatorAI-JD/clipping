✂️ AutoClipper

Stream Clipper

Drop in a stream VOD or long video. AutoClipper scans the audio for hype moments (yelling, laughing, crowd noise), cuts them into vertical 9:16 clips, and sends them to your Review Queue.

Faceless Video Generator

Give it a topic (or let AI pick one). It writes the script, voices it, and renders a captioned vertical video over a background clip.

Review Queue

Nothing posts without you. Approve a clip to download it and copy its caption + hashtags, then upload it to TikTok / Shorts / Reels.

Settings

Stored only in this browser. Without a key, AutoClipper still clips and uses built-in script templates, but videos have no voiceover.
